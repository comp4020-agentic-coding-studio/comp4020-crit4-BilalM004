# Process overview

## What I built
I picked a unique instrument, the handpan, which I enjoyed the sound of. This site is designed to allow for the user to have full freedom to play how they like. There is also reference material (the music book) to try and play (or listen to) popular songs, to hear what they sound like on the instrument. Additionally, the site includes an option to record the notes played, and has 2 other pages, consisting of famous examples and lessons.

## The moments that mattered
This crit being more open-ended, focusing on user experience, rather than having 'test-able' objectives, made me notice the importance of having a clear plan to actually stay on track with. My original idea was feature-first - buttons for famous sounds (notification bells, computer startup chimes, etc) alongside a piano, but the features failed to capture a captivating user experience. Pivoting to a more plan-centered approach, focusing on user experience, rather than features first, changed my plan completely. I was able to develop the foundations of the site first, and then add features as bonuses, not clashing with developing crucial aspects. Having concrete ideas, aligning with my vision, allowed me to give more relevant context to Claude, rather than having it makes these choices. This pivot can be seen in commit [`ede8a31`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-crit4-BilalM004/commit/ede8a31369a76904c5cedd59a80f4949b93a1d7a)

A key user-experience problem encountered was about the site layout. Originally, when 'open book' was clicked, it would open 'music pages' under the book, rather than opening the book itself. This caused visual clutter, pushing down the instrument. Wanting the site to feel more accessible, and usable instantly, without needing any scrolling, I experimented with the layouts, finally opting for having the instrument and music side-by-side ([`dccc9c3`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-crit4-BilalM004/commit/dccc9c3fde0afb700edc115659b2d3aa1a43a799))
